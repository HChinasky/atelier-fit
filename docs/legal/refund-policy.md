# Refund Policy

This is a sample document for the Atelier Fit demo. It is not legal advice.

A real policy states when money or credits are returned. This sample only shows the shape.

## When we refund

- **Charged twice.** The duplicate charge is returned.
- A try-on that fails on our side returns the credit it used.

Refunds go back to the original payment method.
