# How your photo is processed by AI

This is a sample document for the Atelier Fit demo. It is not legal advice.

## How a photo is handled

A photo is sent to a third-party AI provider to generate the try-on image. Our copy of the photo and of the result is deleted within 72 hours; the provider keeps its own copy under its own retention rules. We do not claim that anything is deleted instantly.

## What the AI does not do

The generated image is an illustration, not a guarantee of fit.
