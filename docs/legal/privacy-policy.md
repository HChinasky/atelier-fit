# Privacy Policy

This is a sample document for the Atelier Fit demo. It is not legal advice and must be replaced with a text reviewed by a lawyer before any real use.

## What this page would cover

- which personal data the service collects (e-mail address, uploaded photos, usage data);
- why it is processed and on what legal basis;
- how long it is kept (the retention periods are set in `config/privacy.php`);
- who receives it (the try-on provider, the e-mail relay, analytics);
- how a person asks for access or deletion (see `/data-deletion`).
